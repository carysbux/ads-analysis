Project overview

This project works with a synthetic global ads performance dataset: daily rows with platform, industry, campaign type, spend, revenue, and efficiency metrics. Raw inputs are in data/raw/; cleaned tables and aggregates are in data/processed/.

Analysis notebooks (notebooks/):

  • preprocessing-1.ipynb — cleaning and the processed performance CSV
  • spend-v-revenue-3.ipynb — spend vs revenue curves by quantile bin
  • platform-efficiency-2.ipynb — average CPC & CPA by platform
  • campaign-industry-4.ipynb — best group ROAS per platform × industry (winning campaign type)

Charts below are driven by dashboard/data.json, produced with python3 scripts/build_dashboard_data.py.
